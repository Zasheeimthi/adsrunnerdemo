# Adsrunner demo content review

Reviewed 23 September 2026. This review covers the current index.html and supersedes older descriptions of its content in LANDING_PAGE_REVIEW.md.

## Suggested introduction

Adsrunner brings brand setup, AI ad image creation and campaign information into one workspace. Start with your website or enter your business details manually, create images from a description, and review campaign status, budgets and account connections.

## What changed

- Simplified feature descriptions and replaced vague language with concrete user actions.
- Clarified that manual setup is available without a website.
- Changed the navigation call to action to “Get started” so it describes onboarding accurately.
- Explained ROAS and spelled out click-through rate and conversions.
- Marked dashboard figures and animations as examples or previews.
- Replaced the publishing FAQ with a clear explanation of sample data.
- Removed the unsupported claim about dedicated agency and client workspaces.
- Preserved the requested hero wording, typography, logo, onboarding link and workflow-card navigation.

## Evidence and limits

Verified directly in the product onboarding screen during this review: entering a website to build a brand profile, ad copy and imagery; and the “Skip and set up manually” option. No business details were submitted.

Creative studio, saved images, campaign status/budgets and connection options rely on the existing repository review of previously observed product screens. They were not retested end to end during this review. Listing an integration in the interface does not establish that every feature works for every account.

Before presenting live functionality, confirm which integrations work with the demo account and rehearse image generation and campaign review there. Do not claim automatic campaign publishing, automatic optimization, approval workflows, dedicated client workspaces or guaranteed performance without demonstrating or confirming those features.

All landing-page charts, budgets, conversion counts and return figures are illustrative, including the values shown when switching audience tabs. The landing page is a product preview, not a live dashboard.

## Suggested demo sequence

1. Explain the product using the introduction above.
2. Click “How it works” and demonstrate each of the three preview cards.
3. Explain how a written description and optional reference image guide image creation.
4. Show account connections, noting that available features depend on platform and permissions.
5. Open the FAQ explaining that the figures are examples.
6. Use “Get started” to open real onboarding; continue with prepared demo details only when ready.
