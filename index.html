● GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions.
Clear answers.

A little more about getting started with Adsrunner.

What does Adsrunner do?

Adsrunner helps you build a brand profile, create ad images with AI, review campaign status and budgets, and check your advertising account connections in one workspace.

Do I need a website to get started?

No. You can enter your website address to help Adsrunner create your brand profile, ad text and images, or choose manual setup and enter your business details yourself.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Add a reference image, or choose one from your saved images, to guide the style and layout of the image you create with AI.

Are the numbers on this page real results?

No. The charts, budgets and performance figures on this page are examples that illustrate the workspace. They are not live account data or a promise of advertising results.
